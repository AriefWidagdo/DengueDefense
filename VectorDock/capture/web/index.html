DISTRICT FORECAST

Run a prediction

Forecasts estimate district dengue cases for the next 3 months. Trap counts are shown separately and do not enter these models. Missing values may use the models' saved training statistics; they are not measured inputs.

Select a district represented in the trained model, or enter its BPS code.

The forecast starts in the following month.

How will you provide the monthly data?

Maximum 12 MiB. Include 24 months for the selected district and any available neighboring districts. Leave unknown values blank, not zero.

Required columns and units

date, bps_kode_2025, province, cases, population, rainfall (mm/month), tmin and tmax (monthly mean daily min/max in °C), dry_days (days), oni (°C anomaly), latitude, longitude, mean_mdpl (metres), and is_island (0 or 1).

Provide a row for each of the selected district's 24 months. At least 12 months must have observed case counts. Use the correct province and district code. Neighbor data is optional; missing neighbors reduce coverage.

Enter one district's values below. Neighboring district data is not included with manual entry; the model treats it as missing. Use a CSV to include neighbors.

Enter the district and last input month first.

Forecast result

Research-model estimates, not confirmed case counts. Future accuracy and interval coverage are not established. The result is not saved after leaving or refreshing this page.
